did with it in the simulator. Keep adding entries as documents are read. Local copies of the
documents are in `data/raw/docs/`.

Status key: **applied** (changed the simulator), **pending** (planned change), **context**
(informs explanations or the assumptions panel only), **conflict** (contradicts something
we assumed; see the note).

**Bottom line for R0.** *What current law allows:* bar ADUs beyond the by-right minimum (more or larger §66314 units) in designated areas such as Fire Zones 2–3, the Leave Early Zones or access-constrained streets, with ADU-specific findings (L1, L3, L4); size, height and form limits like Ord. 7,983 (L8); case-by-case SB 9 denials on a specific adverse impact (L5); a moratorium covering by-right units with HCD's approval on an imminent-threat finding (L3, L6); and, our inference, base-zoning reductions offset by upzoning elsewhere (L6). *What would need a change in state law:* a standing area ban on §66323 by-right units (up to three per single-family lot, which is the whole of the Time Study's maximum); an area-wide SB 9 exclusion in Berkeley's VHFHSZ; a unit or population cap, unless §66300(e)(4) is read to cover ordinances.
