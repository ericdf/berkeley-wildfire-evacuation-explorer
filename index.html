Berkeley Wildfire Spread and Access Explorer

Explore how different wildfire scenarios could affect Berkeley's neighborhoods and routes, and see which access vulnerabilities and possible mitigations recur across scenarios.
An educational scenario tool built from public data. Its maps show what happens in these simulations, not what will happen in a real fire: not a prediction, not an official evacuation map or plan, and not an evacuation order or route recommendation. Follow current instructions from Berkeley and Alameda County emergency officials (City's fire weather and evacuation page).
