## What the model does now

- **Network:** County street centerlines in Berkeley with City pavement widths, the City narrow-roads survey, 4 m lidar grades; OpenStreetMap streets beyond the city line (Oakland, Emeryville, Albany, Kensington, El Cerrito, Richmond) up to the edge of the elevation grid, widths assumed from Berkeley medians; the City's 75 diverters, shaped from OpenStreetMap geometry (32 closures, 13 junction splits, 17 one-way pieces, 13 left open), plus 9 OSM bollard and gate closures, including upper Panoramic Way beyond its gate; traffic circles (900 veh/h) at the City's 63 circles and OSM roundabouts. Exits are where streets leave the grid. County records shown wrong by other sources are fixed from a labeled list, `network.segmentCorrections` in `data/assumptions.json`; two entries so far, both County no-vehicle records opened as streets on Eric's decision: the 45 m Bancroft Wy link from the foot of Panoramic Way to Prospect St (SOURCE_NOTES K1–K6) and The Crossways, 67 m between Overlook Rd and Middlefield Rd (K8, where the City segment's network fields side with the County).
- **Parking** (`js/parking.js`): varies by block and seed (none, one side or both sides; partly off the pavement; occasional bad parking jobs), from Eric's street photos and counts of parked cars on 22 hill blocks in the City's 2020 aerial photos. Fuller at night (an assumption). Plan options: status quo, one side only, enforce tight-to-curb, no parking on narrow roads, no parking in the hills.
- **Traffic** (`js/sim.js`): 5 s steps; link and junction capacities; take-turns lanes where parked cars leave one lane; junction delay by road class; reroutes every minute. Gridlock relief lets one car squeeze forward after 3 min if the link ahead has not moved.
- **Demand:** synthetic households from Genasys zone populations (2020 Census blocks for zones the City layer lacks; about 74,700 people outside Berkeley), the Time Study's compliance, departure-time tables, rideshare and returning commuters; plus employees, students, visitors and I-80 and CA-24 through traffic from the study's tables, placed by area.
- **Evacuation time** is measured as the study does: vehicles starting in Order zones, until they leave the order area (Table 7-1 notes).
- **Simple view** (`js/main.js` renderSimple, `js/mitigate.js`, `js/levers.js`; Eric, 2026-11-02; SOURCE_NOTES MS11–MS14, S19). Vocabulary: the *fire scenario* is what Berkeley doesn't control, the *plan* is what it does; a *run* is one simulation; a *pass* runs both plans on the same fire draw and households. Controls on the left, outcomes on the right. Two tabs. **Today's plan**: pick one of four fires (north hills fire from Tilden added 2026-11-02, P30) and a fire pace, "Like 1923" (medium severity) or "Like 1991" (high); watch today's plan (DEFAULT_PLAN, fall night S6, seed 1). **Change the plan**: stack any of the levers in `data/model/mitigations.json` (grouped Before the fire / On the day), compare with today's plan in 15 passes (3 severities × 5 seeds, pool of up to 4 workers; differences under 1% count as noise; verdicts "holds" in every pass or "mostly", ≥75% and never worse). Levers with no reliable effect in the stored table are greyed out with the reason (MS11). **Break down this plan** (2+ changes): each change alone and the plan without each, over the same passes, naming overlaps (MS14). The older questions (`js/presets.js`) are under "Ready-made comparisons". Rerun `node tools/mitigations.mjs` after any model change, and `node tests/stack.mjs`.
- **Engine reach** (`js/sim.js` `engineTimes`, `engineReachTimes`, `engineReachStats`; SOURCE_NOTES ER1–ER3): share of residents in Berkeley's Fire Zones 2–3 whose street an engine can reach from one of Berkeley's 7 stations within the City's 5-minute standard, at ignition, under the plan's parked cars (a home counts at the nearer end of its segment). Results row, two Compare rows, street colors "Engine drive time". The parking question headlines hill residents an engine cannot reach within 5 minutes: 1,510 → 1,010 central with one-side parking, 15 of 15 on every fire. Most of the 1,010 are Panoramic Hill, cut off by parked cars on the lowest block of Panoramic Way (17 ft) in 4 of 5 seeds. At the model's engine speeds almost every home with a route is within 5 minutes, so the measure mostly counts homes parked cars cut off.
- **Traffic operations** (`js/operations.js`, off by default): contraflow on hill exit streets (only where 20 ft stays clear between parked cars), police control of junctions from a shared officer pool, and a Red Flag power shutoff that turns signals into all-way stops (SOURCE_NOTES CF1–CF15).
- **Engine reach** (`js/sim.js` `engineTimes`, `engineReachTimes`, `engineReachStats`; SOURCE_NOTES ER1–ER3): share of residents in Berkeley's Fire Zones 2–3 whose street an engine can reach from one of Berkeley's 7 stations within the City's 5-minute standard, at ignition, under the plan's parked cars (a home counts at the nearer end of its segment). At the model's engine speeds almost every home with a route is within 5 minutes, so the measure mostly counts homes parked cars cut off.
- **Driver information and routing** (`js/guidance.js`; SOURCE_NOTES DG1–DG24): plan setting, Expert view, off by default (Today's model, unchanged). Habit, Ordinary app, Constrained egress guidance (measured against each driver's own habit route; a route is offered only if no riskier under a time-aware forecast of the illustrative fire plus 30 min, and at most 60 s slower or strictly safer; no staging) and Unconstrained system-optimal (comparison only), with a share of drivers who follow (100%, 65%, 50%, 15%; drawn per household from its own stream). "Households worse off" pairs runs household by household; because which car reaches a junction first is partly chance, Compare and the results panel rerun each plan with another junction serving order (`reshuffle`) and report households worse off under both runs ("beyond chance") next to the count chance alone produces. Compare costs 36 runs instead of 18 when the plans differ in routing. `node tests/routing.mjs <scenario> [severity] [seeds]` reproduces the findings.

## Gotchas

- `data/model/*.json` is generated by `tools/build-network.mjs`; rebuild after changing the builder or the lane, parking or capacity assumptions it reads.
- Diverters: `tools/build-network.mjs` section 5b adds nodes and edges (junction splits, connectors, one-way pieces), so edge ids after it differ from the County segment order. Per-device results are in `network.meta.barrierSites`.
- Genasys zone IDs: key on `zone_id` (labels like BER-006). The layer's `zonenum` repeats across cities.
- Zone outlines: Berkeley and Lab zones come from the City layer unless it clips them at the city line; then `tools/build-network.mjs` takes the Genasys outline and prints which zones it swapped (SOURCE_NOTES Z1). A vehicle counts as out of the order area at any node whose 30 m grid cell is in no ordered or warned zone, so any street left in no zone next to an order zone is a free exit; check `evacZone` for nulls after a rebuild.
- OSM roads the street extract leaves out (`highway=service`) can be added from a labeled list, `network.osmAdditions` in `data/assumptions.json` (way, from-node, to-node, why); the build stops if a way or node is gone. The Lab's gate roads are the only entries (SOURCE_NOTES Z5). Lab streets (`labPrivate`) are exit-only at their gates (`network.labGatesExitOnly`).
- Outside Berkeley the hill rules apply to streets at 90 m elevation or higher (`proxy:elevation>=90m` in `fireZoneSrc`); `fireZone` 2 there is that proxy, not a City fire zone. The CAL FIRE layer in the City's FHSZ service was rejected (it rates Hiller Highlands "Moderate").
- `tools/build-network.mjs` step 3b joins OSM to the County network. If you change it, rerun the dead-end check described in SOURCE_NOTES X2 and look for one-way pieces that dead-end (the I-80 ends at Ashby Av and Gilman St were the hard cases).
- Comparing plans relies on the parking draws and household draws depending on the seed, not the plan; keep it that way. Future-growth units are appended after today's households and each draws from its own seeded stream (`js/sim.js` buildHouseholds), so growth and R0 leave today's households unchanged. A carless household of today's draws its rideshare from the shared stream only against donors of today's; where only added households can give a ride it uses a stream of its own (Codex review of PR #2, SOURCE_NOTES R6). Keep every shared-stream draw independent of growth and R0, or the non-resident vehicles drawn after it shift. With maximum growth, R0 in Fire Zones 2–3 reproduces the no-growth run exactly, which is a quick check.
- In `buildHouseholds` (`js/sim.js`), never make a draw conditional on a plan setting, on the shared stream (`this.rand`) or on an added unit's stream. Draws follow the scenario's orders (`this.scenTold`); a number only some plans need comes from the leave-early stream or the per-household `extra` slots (keyed by household index for today's, by unit index among all units for added units). Until 2026-11-01 the leave-early draw ran only when `earlyShare > 0`, which reshuffled every household after the first eligible one (SOURCE_NOTES P24). `node tests/plan-draws.mjs` catches this, with and without growth.
- The error bounds in Assumptions & limits and the results panel (`studyRow` in `js/main.js`) are typed in from the calibration table. Update them whenever calibration changes.
- Simple view remembers the chosen fire in `localStorage` key `bhe-fire`; a fire id that is no longer in `FIRES` falls back to the first fire. Adding a fire means running every question on it (P19 rule) and filling `on` or `off` for each question.
- After every network rebuild (`tools/build-network.mjs`), rerun `node tools/access-constraints.mjs`: the map layer and R0's access-constrained option read its output, which goes stale otherwise. Then rerun `node tools/signals.mjs` (needs `node tools/fetch-city-layers.mjs signals`) and `node tools/contraflow-streets.mjs` (reads access-constraints.json): both store node or edge ids.
- `js/operations.js` must never draw a random number or run when every setting is off (`opsActive`); the activation step calls `route()`, which draws none. Contraflow closes the inbound link (`L.closed`), which `route()`, `enter()` and the commuter path all honor; engines read `ops.cf.blocked`.
